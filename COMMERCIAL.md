## Free under the AGPL

You may use, run, study, change and share Margaya at no cost, including inside a company, as long as you follow the AGPL. The main condition: if you change Margaya and let people use it over a network, you must offer those people your changed source code under the AGPL. Running an unchanged copy only requires keeping the source available, which the in-app **Source code** link already does.

## When you need a commercial licence

You need a commercial licence to use Margaya without the AGPL's conditions, for example to:

- change Margaya and keep your changes private;
- build Margaya, or parts of it, into a closed-source product;
- host Margaya for customers or clients without publishing your source code;
- use Margaya where your company's policy does not allow AGPL software.

If you are unsure which applies to you, ask before you start.
