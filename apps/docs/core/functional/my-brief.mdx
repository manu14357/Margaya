**My brief** is a short personal note about your own work in a workspace. It is off until you turn it on, and it only ever shows what you can already see.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Home** and click **Manage widgets**.
2. Turn on **My brief**, and drag it where you want it.

The setting is personal and applies to this workspace only.

## What it shows

- **Overdue** and **Due today**: your open tasks, using your account time zone.
- **Blocked**: your open tasks waiting on an open blocker (task relation **blocks**). Blockers in projects you can't see are counted, never named.
- **Mentions**: unread mentions from the last 7 days.
- **Changes on tasks you follow**: what other people changed in the last 24 hours on tasks you watch, plus tasks assigned to you unless you muted them.

Click a task to open it. Every list is computed by Margaya; nothing here needs AI.

## Summarize with AI

When AI is turned on for the workspace, **Summarize** writes a few sentences about what needs you first, citing tasks by key. Tasks of projects that have AI turned off, or restricted projects that AI may not read, are left out of the summary but stay in the lists.

## Add it to your email digest

Under **Settings -> Account -> Notifications**, turn on **Add My brief to the digest**. Your daily or weekly email digest then also lists your blocked tasks and changes on tasks you follow since the last digest. It needs a daily or weekly digest, and it's off by default.
