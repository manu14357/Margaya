---
title: Archive and Restore Work
description: Archive tasks, cycles, modules and projects to tidy up without deleting, then find and restore them from Archives
---

Archiving hides finished work from boards, lists and counts while keeping all of it. Nothing is deleted, and restoring puts an item back where it was.

## What you can archive

- **Tasks** - from a task's menu, in bulk, or from a column's menu. See [Save and share views](/core/functional/views#archived-tasks).
- **Cycles** - completed cycles only. See [Plan work in Cycles](/core/functional/cycles#archive-a-cycle).
- **Modules** - completed or cancelled modules only. See [Group work with Modules](/core/functional/modules#archive-a-module).
- **Projects** - from the project's settings, as described below.

A project can also archive its closed tasks on a schedule: see [Automate task cleanup](/core/functional/automations).

## Archive a project

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Projects** and select your project.
3. In the **Danger zone** of the **General** tab, click **Archive project** and confirm.

You need permission to update projects. An archived project is hidden from the sidebar and project lists. Its tasks, cycles and modules are kept, and none of them show up in the workspace Archives, which only lists items from projects that are not archived. They come back together when you restore the project.

An archived project still opens from a link or from the Archives page. A **This project is archived.** bar appears under its header with **Restore** and **View in Archives**. In **Settings**, the **Archive project** row becomes **Restore**.

## Open the Archives pages

- **Workspace:** click **Archives** on the **Projects** page, or run **Go to archives** from the command palette.
- **Project:** open the **...** menu of a project in the sidebar and choose **Archives**, or run **Go to project archives** from the command palette while you are in the project.

Each page has a tab per kind of item with a count: **Tasks**, **Cycles**, **Modules** and, in the workspace only, **Projects**. A tab is hidden when you lack permission to read that kind of item or when the feature is off in every project in scope. Items are listed with the newest archive first and show when they were archived.

On the workspace page, a project filter narrows **Tasks**, **Cycles** and **Modules** to one project.

## Restore an item

- **Tasks** - click **Restore** on a row, or open the task and click **Restore** in the banner "This task was archived ..." at the top of it. Restoring needs permission to update tasks. A restored task returns to the column it was in, with its dates and history intact.
- **Cycles, modules and projects** - click **Restore** on the row, or use the row's menu. **Open** shows the item first.

A row leaves the list as soon as you restore it. If the restore fails, it comes back and an error message appears.

## Restore several tasks at once

On the **Tasks** tab, select tasks and use the bar at the bottom: **Restore** brings them all back. The same bar has **Delete** for people who can delete tasks. Press `Esc` or the close button to clear the selection.

The **Tasks** tab uses the usual toolbar, so you can search, filter and sort archived tasks, for example by when they were archived.

## Delete permanently

Archived items can also be deleted from the Archives pages, after a confirmation. Deleting a cycle or module keeps its tasks in the project and can't be undone. Deleted tasks and projects move to the [trash](/core/functional/trash-and-restore), where they can be restored until they are deleted permanently. These actions need the matching delete permission.

## Tips

- Archive instead of deleting when you might need the history later.
- If a favorite or a link seems to have vanished, check Archives first.
