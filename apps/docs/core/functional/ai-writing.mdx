Margaya's writing help works where you already write: in pages, task descriptions, comments, cycles and projects. Each feature proposes text for you to review. Nothing is changed or created until you choose to apply it.

These features appear only when AI is on for the server, the workspace and the project, and you have the **Use AI** permission. See [AI Setup](/core/functional/ai-setup). When AI is off, the buttons and menu items described here are hidden.

## Edit text with AI

In a page or a task description, select some text and choose **AI** at the start of the formatting bubble. In a page you can also open a block's menu with the handle to the left of it (or `Alt` + `Shift` + `M`) and choose **AI** to work on the whole block.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Improve writing** | Makes the text clearer and more concise, in the same language and tone. |
| **Fix spelling & grammar** | Corrects spelling, grammar and punctuation, and changes nothing else. |
| **Make shorter** | Roughly halves the text and keeps its key points. |
| **Make longer** | Adds detail and explanation that follow from the text, without inventing facts. |
| **Summarize** | Condenses the text into a few sentences or bullets. |
| **Translate to…** | Translates into any of Margaya's 20 interface languages. Code, links and names stay as they are. |
| **Ask AI to…** | Type your own instruction, for example "turn this into a checklist". |

<Screenshot src="ai/editor-ai-menu" alt="The AI menu opened from the formatting bubble, with Improve writing, Fix spelling & grammar, Make shorter, Make longer, Summarize, Translate to… and Ask AI to…" caption="Choose AI at the start of the formatting bubble." />

The suggestion streams into a preview under the text. When it's done you can edit it, then choose:

- **Replace** to swap the selected text for the suggestion.
- **Insert below** to add it after the block, keeping the original.
- **Discard** (or `Esc`) to close the preview without changing anything.
- **Try again** for a new suggestion.

<Screenshot src="ai/editor-ai-preview" alt="An Improve writing preview under the selected callout, with the editable suggestion Sprint 14 scope is frozen. <Info>
On pages that others are editing at the same time, the suggestion is applied as one ordinary edit. Everyone sees it at once, and **Undo** reverts it. If someone else deletes the selected text while you review, **Replace** is no longer offered, but **Insert below** still is.
</Info>

Only the selected text is sent to the model, together with your instruction. You need permission to edit the page or the task.

### Tone

**Improve writing**, **Make shorter**, **Make longer** and **Ask AI to…** can write in a tone:

- **Default** keeps the tone of your text.
- **Professional** is clear, precise and courteous.
- **Casual** is friendly and conversational.

Pick the tone under **Tone** in the AI menu, or switch it in the preview: the suggestion is rewritten in the new tone. Margaya remembers your choice in this browser. **Fix spelling & grammar**, **Summarize** and **Translate to…** don't change the tone.

### Rate a suggestion

Under a finished suggestion, 👍 and 👎 tell your workspace admins whether AI helped. Only the rating and the model are kept, never the text. The rates show on the AI usage page.

## AI in comments

When you write a comment on a task, the ✦ button next to **Attach** opens the same AI actions for your draft: improve, fix, shorten, lengthen, summarize, translate, your own instruction and the tone. The suggestion appears under the comment box, where you can edit it. **Replace draft** puts it in the comment box; nothing is posted until you send the comment yourself. **Discard** (or `Esc`) closes it.

AI in comments needs AI on for the task's project and the permission to comment on the task.

## AI blocks in pages

Type `/` in a page and choose **AI block** (in the **Insert** group). Pick what to generate from the page:

| Preset | What it writes |
|---|---|
| **Summary (TL;DR)** | Two or three sentences, then the key points. |
| **FAQ** | The questions a reader would ask, answered from the page. |
| **Action items** | A checklist of follow-ups, with owners and dates only when the page names them. |
| **Custom prompt** | Anything you ask for, for example "a table of the risks". |

The block is generated from the page as it is in the editor, including edits that are not saved yet. It appears as a proposal with a dashed border where you typed `/`:

- **Accept** inserts it as ordinary page content. **Undo** removes it again.
- **Regenerate** writes a new version.
- **Discard** (or `Esc`) drops it.

If the page changes while the proposal is open, it is marked as out of date; **Regenerate** to include the changes. Co-editors don't see the proposal until you accept it.

## Suggest a page title

Under a page's title, **Suggest a title** proposes one title from the page's content. **Use this title** applies it, ↻ suggests another and **×** closes the suggestion. It needs permission to edit the page.

## Write a new task's description

In the **Create task** dialog, **Write with AI** sits above the description:

- **Draft from title** writes a description from the title and any notes you already typed: a short goal, what is in scope, and acceptance criteria as a checklist. Anything the title doesn't say is left as `[TBD]` rather than invented.
- **Improve** rewrites the description you typed.
- An optional line tells it what to focus on, for example "mention the mobile app".

The text streams into a preview. **Use this** puts it in the description, where you can still edit it; **Try again** writes a new version and **Discard** drops it. It needs AI on for the project and permission to create tasks.

## One-click summaries

Choose **Summarize** (the ✦ button) to get a short summary in a card at the side of the screen:

- **Cycle:** in a cycle's header. It covers progress, risks and blockers, notable changes from the last 7 days, and what needs attention next.
- **Project:** next to the project's name on the board, list and other task views. It covers status and health: open, overdue and unassigned work, what moved recently, and what is stuck or at risk.
- **Page:** in a page's header. It gives a TL;DR with the key points, decisions and open questions.
- **Task:** **Summarize** in the row of actions under a task's description. It tells where the task stands from its description, latest comments and change history: the current state, what was decided, what is open or blocked, and the next step.

<Screenshot src="ai/summary-card" alt="The AI summary card for the Website relaunch project with a verdict, then Status, Risks and Next sections that name tasks by key, and Copy, Save as page and Try again" caption="A project summary, written from the board and its numbers." />

If you can read analytics, cycle and project summaries also use the numbers from [Forecast and Risk](/core/functional/forecast-and-risk). Those dates and percentages are computed by Margaya, not guessed by the model.

In the summary card you can:

- **Copy** the summary as Markdown.
- **Save as page** to keep it as a new page in the project. This needs permission to create pages, with Pages turned on for the project.
- **Save as comment** (task summaries) to post it on the task. This needs permission to comment.
- Rate it with 👍 or 👎.
- **Try again** for a fresh summary, or close the card with **×** or `Esc`.

The summary is never saved unless you choose **Save as page** or **Save as comment**.

## Draft a spec from a task

On a task, choose **Draft a spec** in the row of actions under the description. AI drafts a spec page from the task's title, description, latest comments and related tasks, with these sections:

- **Problem**
- **Goals**
- **Non-goals**
- **Approach**
- **Open questions**

Review the draft, change the title, and choose **Edit** to change the text. Then choose **Create page**. The page is created in the task's project and starts with a link to the task, so it also appears under **Mentioned in pages** on the task. **Discard** closes the draft without creating anything.

<Screenshot src="ai/spec-draft" alt="The Draft a spec dialog for Accessibility audit for the navigation, with the page title and a draft with Problem, Goals, Non-goals, Approach and Open questions sections, and Try again, Discard and Create page" caption="Review the draft, then create the page." />

**Draft a spec** needs permission to create pages, with Pages turned on for the task's project.

## Language

Answers are written in your interface language. Rewrites keep the language of the original text unless you ask for a translation.

## Privacy and limits

- AI only reads what you can see. A page, task, cycle or project in a [restricted project](/core/functional/restricted-projects) you're not a member of is treated as if it doesn't exist. Restricted projects you are a member of are left out unless your workspace admin includes them in the AI settings.
- Text from tasks, comments and pages is passed to the model as data. The model is told never to follow instructions found inside it.
- Each request counts toward the workspace's and your AI limits, and appears on the AI usage page as **Writing help**, **Summaries** or **Spec drafts**. Margaya keeps the counts, never your text or the suggestion (see [AI privacy and data](/core/functional/ai-privacy-and-data)).
- AI can make mistakes. Read the result before you use it.

## API

These features are also available over the REST API. Each one streams Server-Sent Events, or returns a single JSON body when you send `Accept: application/json`:

| Endpoint | What it does |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/ai/writing/assist` | Rewrite a passage of a page, a task or a comment draft (`target.kind` `comment_draft` with the task id), optionally with a `tone`. |
| `POST /api/ai/writing/summarize` | Summarize a cycle, project, page or task. |
| `POST /api/ai/writing/page-block` | Generate an AI block (`tldr`, `faq`, `action_items` or `custom`) from a page. |
| `POST /api/ai/writing/title` | Suggest a page title (JSON only: `{ "title": "…" }`). |
| `POST /api/ai/writing/spec-draft` | Draft a spec from a task. |

API keys need the **AI** area with write access.
