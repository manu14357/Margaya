---
title: Calendar Feeds
description: Subscribe to your tasks, milestones and cycles from any calendar app with a private, read-only link
---

Calendar feeds put your Margaya dates next to your meetings. Each feed is a private link in the standard iCalendar format, so any calendar app that can subscribe to a calendar from a URL can show it. Feeds are read-only: you plan in Margaya, and your calendar follows.

## The feeds you can subscribe to

| Feed | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **My tasks** | Tasks assigned to you that have a start or due date, across every project of the workspace you can see. |
| **Milestones and cycles** | Milestone tasks and cycle date ranges from every project of the workspace you can see. |
| **A project** | Every dated task, milestone and cycle of one project. |

Each feed belongs to you and to one workspace. Your teammates can make their own; nobody can see or manage yours.

## Subscribe to your tasks or milestones

<Screenshot src="account/calendar-feeds" alt="The Subscribe in calendar section of Preferences: My tasks with an active link, its first characters and creation date, and New link and Revoke buttons; Milestones and cycles with No link yet and a Create link button" caption="One feed in use and one without a link." />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your preferences">
    Go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Preferences** and find **Subscribe in calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a link">
    Next to **My tasks** or **Milestones and cycles**, click **Create link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Copy the link from the dialog. It is shown only once; if you lose it, create a new link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your calendar app">
    In your calendar app, choose the option to add or subscribe to a calendar from a URL, and paste the link. In a browser you can also click **Open in calendar app**, which hands the link to the calendar app your system uses for calendar subscriptions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Subscribe to a project

In the **Projects** side panel, open a project's **⋯** menu and choose **Subscribe in calendar**, then click **Create link** and add the link to your calendar app the same way.

## How tasks appear

- Every task is an **all-day event**. Margaya dates have no time of day, so a task with only a due date is one day long, and a task with a start and a due date spans every day between them.
- Days are counted in **your account time zone** (**Settings** → **Account** → **Preferences** → **Time zone**), or the workspace's time zone when you haven't chosen one.
- Milestones start with **◆**, cycles with **↻**, and finished tasks with **✓**.
- Each event links back to its task or cycle in Margaya, and lists the task's key and project.
- Feeds cover the last 90 days and the next year, up to 2,000 events, and leave out archived tasks and anything in the trash.
- Events don't block time: calendar apps show them as free.

Calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule, often every few hours. Margaya suggests hourly, but a change can take longer to show up in your calendar than in Margaya.

## Keep your links private

Anyone with a feed link can read the task titles and dates in it, without signing in. Treat it like a password.

- **New link** gives a feed a new address. The old link stops working at once, and calendars that use it stop updating until you add the new one.
- **Revoke** deletes the feed. Its link stops working at once.
- Settings show each link's first characters, when it was created, and when a calendar last read it, so you can spot a link you don't use any more.

You can see and revoke all your feeds of a workspace under **Settings** → **Account** → **Preferences**, including project feeds you made from the project menu.

## What the feed respects

A feed only ever shows what you could see in Margaya at that moment. Each time a calendar app reads it, Margaya checks again:

- If you leave the workspace or are removed from it, its feeds stop working and are deleted. Joining again doesn't bring the old links back.
- [Restricted projects](/core/functional/restricted-projects) you are not a member of are left out, and a project feed stops working if the project becomes hidden from you or moves to the trash.
- [Guests](/core/functional/guest-access) see only the projects they were added to.
- Cycles appear only for projects that have the Cycles feature turned on.

<Note>
Feeds are one-way. Moving or editing an event in your calendar app doesn't change the task in Margaya.
</Note>

## For developers

Feeds are managed through the [Calendar feeds API](/api-reference/endpoints/calendar-feeds/listCalendarFeeds). Creating and revoking feeds needs a signed-in session; API keys can't create feed links. The feed itself is served at `/api/calendar-feed/{token}/feed.ics` with `text/calendar` content, an `ETag`, and `Cache-Control: private`. Margaya stores only a hash of each link's token.
