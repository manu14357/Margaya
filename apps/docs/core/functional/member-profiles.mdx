Every workspace member has a profile page. It answers "what is this person working on?" without building a view.

## Open a profile

- **Members**: click a member's name in the members list (**Settings** > **Members**).
- **Task assignees**: in a task's properties, hover the assignee faces and choose **View profile** next to a person.
- **Your work**: on the **Summary** tab, click **View your profile** next to your name.

## Overview

The **Overview** tab shows:

- **Header**: avatar, name, email, role and the date they joined the workspace.
- **Numbers**:
  - **Assigned and open**: tasks assigned to them that are not completed or cancelled.
  - **Overdue**: open tasks past their due date.
  - **Completed in 30 days**: their tasks finished in the last 30 days.
  - **Created in 30 days**: tasks they created in the last 30 days.
- **Assigned work by state** and **Open work by priority**: where their work sits right now.
- **Recent activity**: their latest changes and comments, newest first.

Click a number, a state or a priority to open the matching tasks in the **Assigned** tab.

## Assigned

The **Assigned** tab lists every task assigned to them, grouped by state and ordered by due date. Use **Filter** and **Display** and switch between **List**, **Board** and **Spreadsheet**, as on [Your work](/core/functional/your-work). Click a task to open it in a side panel.

## Privacy and permissions

- Only members of the workspace can open a profile, and only for current members. Anyone else gets an error.
- A profile shows only what you can already see: tasks in projects you have access to, never other people's drafts, requests still waiting in intake, archived or trashed tasks, or anything from other workspaces the person belongs to.
- The numbers, the task list and the activity need the **task: read** permission, like any task list.
- The email shown is the one the members list already shows to every member.
