title: Account Security
description: Reset a forgotten password, change it, verify your email, review where you're signed in and leave a workspace
---

Password, email verification and your signed-in devices live under **Settings -> Account -> Security**.

## Forgot your password

When your server can send email and password sign-in is on, the sign-in screen shows **Forgot password?** next to the password field.

1. Select **Forgot password?** and enter your account email.
2. Open the email titled **Reset your Margaya password** and select **Reset password**.
3. Enter a new password twice and select **Set new password**.
4. Sign in with the new password.

- The confirmation looks the same whether or not an account uses that email, so the form never reveals who has an account.
- A reset link works once and expires after **1 hour**. An old or used link shows **This reset link is invalid or has expired**; request a new one from there.
- Setting a new password signs out **every** session of your account, on every device.
- Passwords need at least 8 characters.

<Note>
  Without email configured on the server, the link is not shown. Ask an instance admin to reset access for you.
</Note>

## Change your password

The **Password** section appears when your account signs in with a password (accounts that only use GitHub, Google, Discord or single sign-on don't have one).

1. Enter your **Current password**, then the **New password** twice.
2. Keep **Sign out other sessions** checked (the default) to sign out every other device. Your current device stays signed in.
3. Select **Change password**.

A wrong current password is refused and nothing changes.

## Verify your email

Workspace invitations sent to your address only show in Margaya once the address is verified, so that someone who registers with your email can't see them. Accounts created with a password start unverified; accounts from GitHub, Google and most single sign-on providers, or from an email code, are usually verified already.

1. Open **Invitations** in the Home panel, or **Settings -> Account -> Security**.
2. Under **Verify your email**, select **Send code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from the email (it expires after 5 minutes) and select **Verify**.

Your pending invitations appear right away. The emailed invitation link works whether or not your address is verified. Verification needs email to be configured on the server.

## Active sessions

**Active sessions** lists every device signed in to your account, newest activity first, with:

- the browser and operating system, read from the browser's user agent;
- when the session started and when it was last active (refreshed about once a day of use, so this is approximate);
- the IP address it signed in from, when known;
- a **This device** badge on the session you are using.

Select **Sign out** on a session you don't recognize, or **Sign out all other sessions** to keep only this one. Signed-out devices must sign in again. To end the current session, use **Sign out** in the account menu.

## Leave a workspace

1. Switch to the workspace and go to **Settings -> Workspace -> General**.
2. Under **Danger zone**, select **Leave workspace** and confirm.

You lose access to its projects, tasks and pages at once; someone has to invite you again for you to come back. Margaya then opens another of your workspaces, or workspace setup if you have none left.

The **only owner** of a workspace can't leave it. Transfer ownership to another member first (**Transfer ownership** on the same page), or delete the workspace.

## Sign-in limits

To slow down password guessing, the server limits how often one address can try to sign in, request codes or reset a password. When you hit a limit you'll see **Too many requests**; wait a minute and try again. See [Environment variables](/core/installation/environment-variables#sign-in-rate-limiting) for self-hosting details.
