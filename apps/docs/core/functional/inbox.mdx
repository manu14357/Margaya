---
title: Stay on Top of Your Inbox
description: Read, snooze and archive notifications, choose which tasks you follow, and react to tasks and comments
---

The **Inbox** collects updates on the tasks you care about in one workspace. Open it from **Inbox** in the sidebar, which shows your unread count, or press `n` then `i`. Press `n` then `o` to open the notifications menu from anywhere.

## What reaches your inbox

You are notified when someone:

- assigns you a task or mentions you
- comments on, moves, renames or reschedules a task you follow
- changes the priority of a task you follow
- accepts, declines or closes as duplicate a request you submitted through [Intake](/core/functional/intake)

Due-date reminders and overdue alerts arrive here too. Your own changes never notify you.

## Read and triage notifications

- **All** shows everything; **Mentions** shows only @mentions of you.
- **Filters** narrows the list to **Assigned to me**, **Created by me** or **Subscribed** tasks, and **Unread only** hides what you have read.
- Click a notification to preview its task next to the list. **Open in full page** opens the task itself.
- Use a notification's actions to **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Archive** it, or **Snooze** it.
- **Mark all as read** marks everything the current list shows.

Most actions offer **Undo** for a few seconds.

## Snooze and archive

**Snooze** hides a notification until **1 day**, **3 days**, **5 days**, **1 week** or **2 weeks** from now, or a **Custom…** date and time. When the time comes, it returns to the top of your inbox as unread. **Unsnooze** brings it back right away.

**Archive** moves a notification out of the way without deleting it. Archiving a snoozed notification also ends the snooze.

Switch between the **Inbox**, **Snoozed** and **Archived** lists with the view menu. In **Archived**, **Move to inbox** restores a notification.

**Clear all notifications** in the notifications menu deletes every notification in the workspace for good.

## Choose which tasks you follow

You follow (are subscribed to) a task automatically when you create it, are assigned to it, comment on it or are mentioned in it. Subscribers hear about status changes, comments, time entries, and priority, due date and title changes.

To change it, open the task and click **Subscribe** or **Unsubscribe**. The button tells you why you are subscribed and how many people follow the task.

Unsubscribing also stops due-date reminders, even on tasks assigned to you. A new mention or assignment still notifies you and subscribes you again.

## React to tasks and comments

React to a task or a comment instead of writing "+1". Click **Add reaction** and pick one of eight reactions: thumbs up, thumbs down, smile, boom, confused, heart, airplane or eyes. Hover a reaction to see who used it, and click your own reaction again to remove it.

## Get notifications elsewhere

The inbox is always the source of truth. To also receive notifications by email, ntfy, Gotify or a webhook, see [Account notifications](/core/functional/account-notifications).

## Permissions

The inbox is personal: each person only sees and changes their own notifications. You are only notified about tasks you can see.
