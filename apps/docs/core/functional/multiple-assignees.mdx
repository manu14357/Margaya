---
title: Assign Multiple People to a Task
description: Add up to 20 assignees to a task, keep their order, and pick people by search or number keys
---

Tasks can have more than one assignee. Assignees keep the order they were added in, so the first assignee (still available as a single value for older integrations) stays predictable.

## Assign people to a task

1. Open a task and select its **Assignees** property.
2. Pick anyone from the workspace member list. Rows show up to 9 with number-key shortcuts; once a project has more members than that, a search field appears instead.
3. Toggle as many people as you need, up to 20 per task.

<Screenshot src="tasks/assignee-picker" alt="The Assignees picker on a task with Priya Raman and Elena Marsh ticked, the other members numbered 3 to 5, and Clear assignees at the bottom" caption="Ticked people are assigned; number keys pick a row." />

Assignees keep the order they had when you opened the picker, so rows don't jump around as you make selections; every change you make is applied at once when you close the picker.

## Change or clear assignees

- Toggling someone off removes just that person; everyone else stays assigned.
- Use **Clear assignees** to unassign everyone on the task at once.
- People who are no longer workspace members show as "No longer a member" and can be removed but not re-added.

## Bulk assign across tasks

Select several tasks in Board or List view to reach the bulk toolbar's assignee actions: **Add assignee** adds one person to every selected task, **Remove assignee** lists whoever is on any selected task so you can take them off all of them, and **Clear assignees** removes everyone from every selected task at once. Each action reports back how many of the selected tasks actually changed.

## Permissions

Changing who is assigned to a task needs the **task:assign** permission, which is separate from the general task update permission. In the built-in roles, only Admin and Owner include task:assign by default; Viewer and Member do not, so a workspace admin needs to grant it through a custom role if members should be able to reassign tasks themselves. Anyone who can only read a task still sees its assignees. Every assignee must be a member of the workspace.

## Tips

- Use one primary assignee plus collaborators rather than assigning everyone "just in case"; it keeps ownership clear.
- The search field (shown once a project has more than 8 members) filters as you type, so you don't need to scroll a long member list.
- Bulk-assign from List view when you need to add a reviewer or collaborator to many tasks at once.
