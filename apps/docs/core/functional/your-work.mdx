title: Track Your Work
description: See what is assigned to you, what you created and what you watch, across every project in the workspace
---

**Your work** gathers your tasks from every project in the workspace into one page. Open it from **Personal** > **Your work** in the sidebar, or press `g` then `y`.

## Summary

The **Summary** tab shows your numbers at a glance:

- **Assigned and open** - tasks assigned to you that are not completed or cancelled
- **Overdue** - open tasks past their due date
- **Due this week**
- **Completed in 30 days**
- **Created** and **Subscribed** - tasks you created or watch

Click a number to open the matching list. **View your profile** next to your name opens your [member profile](/core/functional/member-profiles), the page teammates see. Below the numbers, **Workload** breaks your open work down by priority and by project, and **Recent activity** lists your latest changes. Click a priority or a project to open those tasks.

## Assigned, Created and Subscribed

The other tabs list tasks by how they relate to you:

- **Assigned** - tasks you are an assignee of
- **Created** - tasks you created
- **Subscribed** - tasks you watch, see [Inbox and notifications](/core/functional/inbox)

Each list is grouped by status group and ordered by due date. Use **Filter** and **Display** to narrow it down, and switch between **List**, **Board** and **Spreadsheet**, just as in [views](/core/functional/views). Click a task to open it in a side panel without leaving the page.

## Permissions

Your work only shows tasks in projects you can already see. It needs no extra permission.

## Tips

- Start the day on **Overdue** and **Due this week**.
- To share one of these lists, start from the **Assigned to me** or **Created by me** tab under **All tasks** and save it as a [view](/core/functional/views).
