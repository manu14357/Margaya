title: Review Requests with Intake
description: Collect requests in a review queue, then accept, decline, snooze or close them as duplicates before they reach the board
---

Intake gives a project a queue for incoming requests, such as bug reports or ideas from people outside the team. Requests wait there for review and never clutter the board, search or reminders until someone accepts them.

## Turn on Intake

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Projects** and select your project.
3. Open the **Features** tab.
4. Turn on **Intake**.

The project gets an **Intake** tab. Its badge shows how many requests are waiting for review.

## Submit a request

1. Open the project's **Intake** tab and click **New request**. You can also run **New request** from the command palette.
2. Describe what you need in the title, add details, steps to reproduce or links, and set a **Priority** if you know it.
3. Click **Submit request**. Turn on **Create more** to submit several in a row.

<Screenshot src="intake/new-request-dialog" alt="The New request dialog with the title field What do you need?, a details field, a Priority button, the Create more switch and Submit request" caption="Describe the request and submit it for review." />

While a request is open, its requester can still **Edit request** or **Withdraw request**. Requests can also be submitted through the [API](/api-reference/endpoints/intake/createIntakeItem); they show "via API". People outside the workspace can send requests [by email](#receive-requests-by-email) or through a [public request form](#share-a-public-request-form).

### Paste screenshots

Paste one or more screenshots (PNG, JPEG or WebP) anywhere in the **New request** dialog. They wait beside the form and are attached to the request once you submit it. Attaching needs permission to edit tasks (**task:update**); without it, pasting does nothing.

## Capture requests with AI

When AI is on for the workspace and the project (see [Set up AI](/core/functional/ai-setup)), the Intake tab also has a **Capture** button. It turns raw evidence into well-written requests:

- **Screenshots**: paste, drop or choose up to 6 images. AI writes one request with a title, a summary, steps to reproduce, the expected and the actual behavior, and the environment when it's visible.
- **Recording**: choose a screen recording. Margaya takes 5 still frames from it in your browser and sends only those; the video never leaves your device.
- **Transcript**: paste meeting notes or a transcript. AI proposes one request per action item, up to 10, and suggests an owner when the notes clearly give the item to a project member.

<Screenshot src="ai/capture-transcript" alt="The New requests from a capture dialog on the Transcript tab, with notes from a beta sync meeting pasted in and Propose requests" caption="Paste meeting notes, screenshots or a recording." />

Each proposal also suggests a **Priority** (from the severity it sees) and existing workspace labels. Nothing is created until you confirm: edit any title or description, change the priority, untick labels or the owner, and leave out proposals you don't want. **Create requests** submits the rest as ordinary pending requests, adds the labels and owner, and attaches the screenshots or frames to the request.

<Screenshot src="ai/capture-review" alt="Review proposed requests with three requests from the meeting notes, each with an editable title and description, a priority with its reason, suggested labels and an owner such as Theo Okafor, and Create requests (3)" caption="Edit or leave out any proposal before you create it." />

In the **New request** dialog, **Draft from images** does the same for pasted screenshots: it fills in the title, description and priority for you to review before you submit.

Privacy and limits:

- Images are redrawn in your browser and checked again on the server, so location, camera and other metadata (EXIF, XMP, IPTC) is removed before AI sees them. Each image may be up to 3.75 MB, 9 MB together.
- Images and notes count as private data. They follow your instance's AI routing, including local-only mode. Restricted projects are left out unless the workspace includes them in AI.
- Text inside screenshots and transcripts is treated as data: AI never follows instructions found there, and it has no tools, so it can't change anything.
- Prompts and answers aren't stored; only usage counts are kept.

Capture needs the **Use AI** permission (`ai:use`) and Intake **create**. Adding labels needs **label:update**, assigning an owner needs **task:assign**, and attaching images needs **task:update**. It's also available through the API as [`POST /ai/capture/{projectId}`](/api-reference/endpoints/ai/aiCaptureAnalyze).

An [AI agent](/core/functional/ai-agents#new-intake-requests) can also run on every new request, for example to comment with a short triage before anyone looks. It never accepts, declines or moves a request.

## Receive requests by email

Each project with Intake can have its own forwarding address. Mail sent or forwarded there becomes a pending request: the subject is the title, the message is the description, and attachments are kept as task attachments. When the original sender replies to the same thread, the reply is added to the request as a comment instead of opening a new one.

1. Go to **Settings**, open **Projects** and select your project.
2. Open **Intake sources**.
3. Under **Email**, turn on **Accept requests by email**.
4. Copy the **Forwarding address** and share it, or forward a shared inbox such as support@ to it.

<Screenshot src="intake/intake-sources-email" alt="The Email section of Intake sources for Mobile app with Accept requests by email on, the forwarding address with Copy and New address, Only accept these domains listing two domains, Never accept these domains listing one, Save, and the size limits" caption="The project's forwarding address and the domains it accepts." />

The address appears when your server has an intake mailbox; your administrator sets it up as described in [Set up email intake](/core/installation/email-intake). Without one, connect the project's own mailbox under **Project mailbox**: enter the IMAP server, user name and password, click **Save**, use **Test connection**, then turn on **Check this mailbox**. Margaya checks it every two minutes and files every unread message into this project. The password is stored encrypted and is never shown again.

To keep the queue clean, Margaya ignores:

- auto-replies, out-of-office notices, bounces and bulk or mailing-list mail, so it never answers itself in a loop;
- messages your mail server already marked as spam;
- senders from **Never accept these domains**, and, when **Only accept these domains** is filled in, every other domain;
- the same message twice, more than 10 messages an hour from one sender, and more than 200 an hour for the project;
- messages over the size limit (25 MB by default). Attachments over the per-file limit, or beyond the maximum count, are listed in the request instead of attached.

Email requests show "via Email" with the sender's name and address, which only people who can read the whole queue see. **New address** replaces the forwarding address if it leaks; mail to the old one is no longer filed. Attachments aren't scanned for malware, so open them with the same care as any email attachment.

## Share a public request form

A request form is a public page where anyone with the link can send a request, without an account. It shows only the heading, text and fields you choose, never your workspace, project or tasks.

1. Go to **Settings**, open **Projects**, select your project and open **Intake sources**.
2. Under **Request form**, set the **Heading**, an **Introduction** and the **Confirmation message** people see after sending.
3. Choose whether **Description**, **Email address** and **Name** are optional, required or hidden, and tick any **Custom fields** the form should ask for. Their names and options become public. Click **Save**.
4. Turn on **Open the form** and share the **Form link**.

<Screenshot src="intake/intake-sources-form" alt="The Request form section of Intake sources with Open the form on, the Form link with Copy, Open and New link, the heading Report a problem with the Mobile app, an introduction, Description and Name optional, Email address required, no custom fields, and a confirmation message" caption="Choose what the form shows and asks, then share the link." />

<Screenshot src="intake/public-request-form" alt="The public request form Report a problem with the Mobile app, with its introduction, a filled-in Title, an empty Description and Your name, a filled-in Your email marked required, and Send request" caption="What people see when they open the form link." />

Submissions land in Intake as pending requests marked "via Form". The form keeps bots out without a captcha: a hidden trap field that people never see, a minimum time to fill it in, and limits of 10 requests an hour and 30 a day per network, and 100 an hour per form. **New link** replaces the link; the old one stops working. The form is unavailable while the server turns public sharing off, Intake is off, or the project is archived.

Managing intake sources needs the **project:share** permission, the same one that publishes a public board.

## Review the queue

The **Open** tab lists requests waiting for a decision; **Closed** lists accepted, declined and duplicate ones. Search by title, **Filter** by status, priority, source (in app, API, public board, email or form) or who submitted it, and **Sort** by submission date, last activity or task key. Requests that came from outside the app show their source in the list.

Each request has a status:

- **Pending** - waiting for review
- **Snoozed** - hidden until the snooze ends
- **Accepted** - added to the board
- **Declined** - closed without adding it to the board
- **Duplicate** - already covered by another task

Select a request to read it, see its activity and set its **Properties** (priority, assignee, labels and due date) before you decide.

<Screenshot src="intake/intake-queue" alt="The Intake tab of Mobile app with four open requests and MOB-10 Show the build number in Settings selected, showing its description, activity, properties and the Accept, Decline and Snooze buttons" caption="Select a request to review it and decide." />

## Accept, decline, snooze or mark as duplicate

- **Accept** moves the request onto the board. Pick the **Column** it lands in, or **Backlog**. From then on it is an ordinary task, and its details show which request it came from. If the request has no assignee and the project has a [default assignee](/core/functional/personalize-your-workspace#project-lead-and-default-assignee), it is assigned to them.
- **Decline** closes the request without adding it to the board. Add an optional reason: the requester sees it in their notification.
- **Snooze** hides the request until **Tomorrow**, **In 3 days**, **Next week**, **In 2 weeks** or a date you pick. It comes back as pending on its own; **Unsnooze** brings it back now.
- **Mark as duplicate** closes the request in favor of an existing task in the same project. Search for that task by key or title. When AI is on for the project, **Suggested by AI** lists likely duplicates first, each with a short reason; pick one to mark the request as its duplicate.

<Screenshot src="intake/accept-request" alt="The Accept MOB-10 dialog with the Column picker set to To Do and the Accept button" caption="Choose the column the accepted task lands in." />

**Reopen** puts a declined or duplicate request back into review. The requester is notified in their [inbox](/core/functional/inbox) when a request is accepted, declined or marked as a duplicate.

## Permissions

Intake uses the workspace **Intake** permissions:

- **create** - submit requests. Without **read**, you only see your own requests.
- **read** - see every request in the queue.
- **triage** - accept, decline, snooze, mark as duplicate and reopen.

Every built-in role, Viewer included, has **create** and **read**. Members, Admins and Owners also have **triage**.

Deleting someone else's request needs **task:delete**, and editing it needs **task:update**.

## Tips

- Share the Intake tab with people who report issues but shouldn't change the board directly: the Viewer role can submit requests.
- Snooze requests that depend on something else instead of leaving them pending.
- Always add a reason when you decline, so the requester knows what happens next.
