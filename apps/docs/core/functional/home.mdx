**Home** is your personal start page in a workspace. Open it from **Home** in the sidebar, or press `g` then `h`. It greets you, shows cards for your **Inbox**, **Your work** and **Stickies**, and below them your widgets: **Quick links**, **Recents** and **Stickies**.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/home" alt="The Home page of the Studio North workspace with the greeting, the Inbox, Your work and Stickies cards, quick links, recent items and three stickies" caption="Home brings your inbox, your work, links, recent items and notes together." />

## Get started

A new workspace shows a **Get started** checklist on Home: create a project, invite your team, set up the workspace and personalize your account. It disappears once the workspace has a project and a second member, or when you click **Not now**.

## Manage widgets

1. Click **Manage widgets** at the top right of Home.
2. Turn each widget on or off.
3. Drag widgets by their handle to change their order, then click **Close**.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/home-manage-widgets" alt="The Manage widgets dialog with switches for Quick links, Recents and Stickies" caption="Turn widgets on or off and drag them into the order you like." />

**My brief** is off until you turn it on here; see [My brief](/core/functional/my-brief).

Your widget settings are personal and apply to this workspace only. If you turn every widget off, Home shows how to turn them back on.

## Quick links

Keep links you open often, such as docs, dashboards or designs, one click away.

- Click **Add link**, paste a **URL** and optionally give it a **Title**. Links without `http://` or `https://` get `https://` added.
- Each link's menu can **Edit**, **Open in new tab**, **Copy link** or **Delete** it.
- The same page can't be saved twice, and you can keep up to 50 quick links per workspace.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/home-add-link" alt="The Add link dialog with a roadmap URL and the title Public roadmap filled in" caption="A quick link needs only a URL; the title is optional." />

## Recents

**Recents** lists the tasks, projects, pages and views you opened lately, newest first. The **Recent** list in the Home side panel shows the same items. Use the **All**, **Tasks**, **Projects** and **Pages** tabs to narrow it, and **Show more** to go further back. Opening a task from Recents shows it in a side panel over Home.

## Stickies

Stickies are private notes for ideas, to-dos and reminders. Only you can see your stickies.

- Click **Add sticky** in the widget, or open **Stickies** in the Home side panel (or press `g` then `s`) to see them all.
- Type to write; your changes save automatically. Press `/` in a sticky for formatting commands. Stickies hold text only, not files.
- **Change color** picks a background, **Search stickies** finds a note by its text, and **Delete sticky** removes it for good.
- On the Stickies page, drag a sticky to reorder it.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/stickies" alt="The Stickies page showing a checklist sticky, a reminder and a meeting note in different colors" caption="The Stickies page shows every note; only you can see them." />

You can keep up to 500 stickies per workspace.

## Permissions

Home, quick links and stickies are personal. They need no workspace permission, and nobody else can see yours. Recents only list items you can still open.
