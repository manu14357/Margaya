**Home** is your personal start page in a workspace. Open it from **Home** in the sidebar, or press `g` then `h`. It greets you, shows cards for your **Inbox**, **Your work** and **Stickies**, lists what's **Due today**, and below that shows your widgets: **Quick links**, **Recents** and **Stickies**.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/home" alt="The Home page of the Studio North workspace with the greeting, the Inbox, Your work and Stickies cards, the tasks due today, quick links and recent items" caption="Home brings your inbox, your work, links, recent items and notes together." />

## Due today

**Due today** lists the open tasks assigned to you that are due today, across every project, with the most urgent first. It shows up to 6 tasks, and **View all** opens the rest in [Your work](/core/functional/your-work). Click a task to open it in a side panel. When nothing is due, it says so. This section is always there and isn't one of the widgets you can turn off.

## Get started

A new workspace shows a **Get started** checklist on Home: create a project, invite your team, set up the workspace and personalize your account. It disappears once the workspace has a project and a second member, or when you click **Not now**.

## Manage widgets

1. Click **Manage widgets** at the top right of Home.
2. Turn each widget on or off.
3. Drag widgets by their handle to change their order, then click **Close**.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/home-manage-widgets" alt="The Manage widgets dialog with switches for Quick links, Recents, Stickies, Risks, My brief and Pinned reports" caption="Turn widgets on or off and drag them into the order you like." />

**My brief** is off until you turn it on here; see [My brief](/core/functional/my-brief). **Pinned reports** is off until you pin your first report. **Risks** and **Pinned reports** are only listed if you can read analytics.

Your widget settings are personal and apply to this workspace only. If you turn every widget off, Home shows how to turn them back on.

## Quick links

Keep links you open often, such as docs, dashboards or designs, one click away.

- Click **Add link**, paste a **URL** and optionally give it a **Title**. Links without `http://` or `https://` get `https://` added.
- Each link's menu can **Edit**, **Open in new tab**, **Copy link** or **Delete** it.
- The same page can't be saved twice, and you can keep up to 50 quick links per workspace.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/home-add-link" alt="The Add link dialog with a roadmap URL and the title Public roadmap filled in" caption="A quick link needs only a URL; the title is optional." />

## Recents

**Recents** lists the tasks, projects, pages and views you opened lately, newest first, starting with the latest 5. The **Recent** list in the Home side panel shows the same items. Use the **All**, **Tasks**, **Projects** and **Pages** tabs to narrow it, and **Show more** to go further back. Opening a task from Recents shows it in a side panel over Home.

## Pinned reports

**Pinned reports** shows saved [Analytics reports](/core/functional/analytics#save-a-report) as charts on your Home. It only appears once you pin something, so Home stays as it was until then.

1. Open **Analytics** and go to the **Tasks** tab.
2. Under **Custom chart**, click **Reports** and open the **...** menu of a report.
3. Choose **Pin to Home**.

<Screenshot src="analytics/home-pinned-report" alt="The Pinned reports widget on Home with the report Open work by assignee as a bar chart, an unpin button on its card and an Open Analytics button" caption="A pinned report on Home, with the same chart as in Analytics." />

- You can pin up to 6 reports. Pins are yours alone, and pinning a report someone else shared doesn't change it for them.
- Each card has an unpin button, and **Open Analytics** takes you to the full reports. Unpinning the last report turns the widget off.
- A card shows at most 8 categories, and says "Showing 8 of 20. Analytics has the rest." when there are more. Charts over dates show every point.
- Numbers come from what you may see, so they can differ from a colleague's. A report that was deleted or made private drops off your Home.
- You need the workspace **Analytics** permission (**read**). Without it, nothing is shown.

Only saved reports from **Custom chart** can be pinned. Other charts and tables in Analytics can't.

## Stickies

Stickies are private notes for ideas, to-dos and reminders. Only you can see your stickies.

- Click **Add sticky** in the widget, or open **Stickies** in the Home side panel (or press `g` then `s`) to see them all.
- Type to write; your changes save automatically. Press `/` in a sticky for formatting commands. Stickies hold text only, not files.
- **Change color** picks a background, **Search stickies** finds a note by its text, and **Delete sticky** removes it for good.
- On the Stickies page, drag a sticky to reorder it.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/stickies" alt="The Stickies page showing a checklist sticky, a reminder and a meeting note in different colors" caption="The Stickies page shows every note; only you can see them." />

You can keep up to 500 stickies per workspace.

## Permissions

Home, quick links and stickies are personal. They need no workspace permission, and nobody else can see yours. **Due today** and Recents only list items you can still open. Pinned reports also need the **Analytics** permission.
