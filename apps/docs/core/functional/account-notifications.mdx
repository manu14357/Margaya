Margaya can keep the normal in-app notification inbox, play a sound when a notification arrives, and forward matching notifications to external channels for the signed-in user.

You can configure this under:

1. Open **Settings**
2. Open **Account**
3. Open **Notifications**

## Available channels

Users can forward matching notifications through:

- `Push notifications`
- `Email`
- `ntfy`
- `Gotify`
- `Custom webhook`

These settings are personal to the signed-in account. They do not change notification routing for other users in the workspace.

## Choose what notifies you

**Notify me about** decides which task events create notifications at all, in the inbox and on every channel:

- **Task assignments**
- **Comments and mentions**: new comments on tasks you're assigned to or subscribed to, replies in threads you started or replied in, and direct @mentions
- **Task status changes** on tasks you're assigned to or subscribed to
- **Other task changes**: priority, due date and title changes on tasks you follow
- **Due-date reminders**, with **Remind me before the due date** set between 1 hour and 30 days

Select **Save notification types** to apply your choices.

## Notification sounds

**Sounds** at the top of the page plays a short sound when a new notification reaches you while Margaya is open in your browser, in any tab, even in the background.

- **Play sounds** turns them on or off for your account. They are on by default, for the notifications that need you.
- **Volume** applies to every sound.
- Pick a sound for each kind of notification, or **None** for silence. Choosing a sound plays it, and the play button next to it plays it again.

| Kind | What it covers | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mentions and assignments** | someone @mentions you or assigns you a task | Chime |
| **Agent needs you** | an AI agent asks you a question or waits for your approval | Knock |
| **Replies to you** | replies in comment threads you're in, and an agent's answer to your request | Pop |
| **Everything else** | other comments, status changes, reminders and updates | None |

The built-in sounds are Chime, Pop, Ding, Soft bell, Knock and Marimba. They are generated in the browser, so they need no downloads and work the same on self-hosted installs.

- **Quiet hours** silences sounds between two times in your account's time zone. The range can cross midnight, for example 22:00 to 08:00.
- **Mute sounds on this device** silences only the browser you're using, for example a work laptop in meetings. Every other setting here is saved to your account and follows you to every browser; this one stays on the device.

In the [desktop app](/core/functional/desktop-app#notification-sound), the same settings decide whether a notification makes a sound, and the app plays it with the native notification instead of the page, so you hear it once. On Windows and Linux that's the sound picked here, at your volume; on macOS it's your system's notification sound.

To keep sounds calm:

- a burst of notifications (a bulk change, for example) plays one sound, then sounds pause for a few seconds;
- with several Margaya tabs open, only one of them plays;
- nothing plays while voice mode listens or speaks;
- notifications that arrive while you're offline are added to the inbox without a sound when you reconnect.

Sounds follow **Notify me about** and muted tasks: a notification you turned off never sounds. The inbox and its unread badge update as before, so sound is never the only signal.

## How it works

Margaya already stores in-app notifications in the notification inbox. Account notification delivery adds a separate preference layer on top of that behavior.

Important rules:

- the in-app inbox remains the source of truth
- outbound delivery only applies to newly created notifications
- historical notifications are not replayed
- delivery failures do not block notification creation
- delivery can be scoped by workspace and, optionally, by selected projects

## Stored secrets

Webhook signing secrets and ntfy and Gotify tokens are stored encrypted. Your instance needs `NOTIFICATION_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEY` for that (see [environment variables](/core/installation/environment-variables#notifications)). If it is not set, saving a token or secret fails with a message asking your administrator to set it, while every other notification setting keeps working.

## Global channels

The **Delivery channels** section of the Notifications page includes separate connection cards for:

- `Push notifications`
- `Email`
- `Gotify`
- `ntfy`
- `Custom webhook`

Configure each channel once at the account level, then decide which workspaces are allowed to use it.

### Push notifications

Push sends alerts to your phone or computer, even when Margaya is closed. **Notify me about** decides what is sent.

Push needs three things:

- Your server has push set up. Without it, the card says "Push isn't set up on this server." If you run the server, see [Web push](/core/installation/environment-variables#web-push).
- A current browser, on an HTTPS address.
- On iPhone and iPad, Margaya added to your Home Screen. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Margaya from there before you turn push on.

To turn it on:

1. On the device you want alerts on, open **Settings** > **Account** > **Notifications**.
2. Under **Push notifications**, turn on **On this device**.
3. Allow notifications when your browser asks. If you refuse, allow them in your browser's settings and reload the page.

<Screenshot src="account/push-notifications" alt="The Push notifications card with the On this device switch and its hint, and Your other devices listing one phone with a Remove button" caption="Push is turned on per device." />

Each device is separate. **Your other devices** lists the rest of your devices, and **Remove** stops push to one of them.

- An account can have 10 devices. Adding another removes the oldest.
- Signing out turns push off on that device, so the next person doesn't get your alerts.
- Turning push on in a browser that another account had set up moves it to your account.

Push also ends when your account is signed out elsewhere, so a lost device stops showing alerts:

- **Sign out all other sessions**, changing your password with **Sign out other sessions** checked, and changing your email turn push off on your other devices. The device you did it on keeps push.
- A forgotten-password reset, and an administrator signing you out everywhere, banning you, or resetting your password or two-factor, turn it off on all your devices.

Turn push on again on any device you still use. Signing out a single session under **Active sessions** doesn't stop push, because Margaya can't tell which device that session was. Use **Remove** under **Your other devices** as well.

No workspace rule is needed: push covers every workspace you have no [rule](#workspace-delivery-rules) for. Where you have a rule, it must be active and include the project, with **Push notifications** on.

Page notifications reach the inbox and push, but not the other channels.

A push shows a short title and one fixed line in your language, such as who commented on which task. It names the task or page, but leaves out anything someone wrote, because a push can show on a lock screen: comment text, an agent's answers and questions, what an approval is about, an automation's message and the reason a request was declined. Tapping it opens the task or page, or otherwise the inbox, where you can read the rest. **Quiet hours** only silence sounds, not push.

Your browser may also offer to install Margaya as an app. Margaya has no install button of its own, and installing doesn't add offline use.

### Email

Email delivery uses the signed-in account email address.

Requirements:

- the user must have an email on the account
- SMTP must be configured on the server

### ntfy

ntfy requires:

- a server URL
- a topic
- optionally, a bearer token

### Gotify

Gotify requires:

- a server URL
- an application token (bearer token)

Setup notes:

- create an application in your Gotify server before connecting Margaya
- paste the base server URL, for example `https://gotify.example.com`
- no extra topic or path is required because Margaya sends directly to the Gotify message API using the application token
- if your Gotify server uses a custom port or TLS, include that in the server URL

### Custom webhook

Custom webhook requires:

- a destination URL
- optionally, a signing secret

When a signing secret is configured, Margaya sends an `X-Margaya-Signature` header containing the `HMAC-SHA256` hex digest of the raw JSON body.

### Batched emails

Notification emails are batched. Instead of one email per change, Margaya waits a few minutes after your first new notification and then sends one email with everything that happened in the meantime, grouped by task: each task's title and link, followed by a short list of what changed and who changed it. A batch with a single change looks like an ordinary notification email.

- The wait is set by your administrator with `EMAIL_NOTIFICATION_BATCH_WINDOW_SECONDS` (5 minutes by default; `0` sends every email immediately). See [environment variables](/core/installation/environment-variables#notifications).
- Your settings are checked again when the batch is sent. A notification type you turned off in the meantime, a task you unsubscribed from, a workspace you left, and a task that was deleted, archived or moved back to triage are all left out. If nothing is left, no email is sent.
- Only email waits. The in-app inbox, live updates, push, ntfy, Gotify and webhooks are delivered immediately.
- Sign-in codes, password resets, invitations and security emails are never batched.

### Email digest

Under **Email digest** you can also ask for a summary email: **Off** (the default), **Daily**, or **Weekly** on a day you choose. It is sent at the hour you pick (08:00 by default) in your own time zone. For every workspace you belong to, it lists:

- your open assigned tasks that are overdue or due within the next 7 days
- the mentions you received since the last digest
- how many inbox notifications arrived since the last digest and are still unread

Only tasks and notifications you can still see are included, and workspaces you paused in your workspace rules are left out. When there is nothing to report, no digest is sent. Each digest ends with a **Manage notification settings** link.

<Screenshot src="account/email-digest" alt="The Email digest section with Send a digest, the time it is sent and Save digest" caption="A daily or weekly summary email." />

The digest is separate from the email channel switch: you can turn per-notification emails off and still receive a daily digest. It needs an email address on your account and SMTP on the server.

## Workspace delivery rules

Below the global channel cards, Margaya shows workspace delivery rules.

Each workspace rule can:

- be active or paused
- enable or disable `Email`, `Gotify`, `ntfy`, `Custom webhook` and `Push notifications`
- apply to `All projects`
- or apply to `Selected projects` only

This lets a user receive notifications from one workspace in ntfy, another by email, and ignore the rest.

## Supported notification sources

V1 forwards the same notification types that currently create inbox notifications in Margaya.

That includes:

- `task_created`
- `workspace_created`
- `task_status_changed`
- `task_assignee_changed`
- `time_entry_created`

When your workspace uses AI, these are forwarded too:

- `ai_agent_reply`: an [agent](/core/functional/ai-agents) answered your request outside the task, or has a question for you
- `ai_approval_requested`: an agent proposed a change that waits for your approval
- `ai_budget_warning`: the workspace has used 80% of its monthly AI budget (only to people with **Manage AI**, once a month)

If Margaya cannot resolve a notification to a workspace or project context, that notification stays inbox-only.

## Reachability and security

For security, Margaya validates outbound HTTP destinations before saving or sending.

Private or non-routable destinations are rejected, including examples like:

- `localhost`
- `127.0.0.1`
- `10.x.x.x`
- `172.16.x.x` to `172.31.x.x`
- `192.168.x.x`

This applies to:

- `ntfy` server URLs
- `Gotify` server URLs
- custom webhook URLs

If you are using a self-hosted ntfy instance, Gotify instance, or webhook receiver, it must be reachable from the API with an accepted public or routable hostname.

If your receiver only exists on a private network, set `MARGAYA_ALLOW_PRIVATE_WEBHOOK_DESTINATIONS=true` on the API to bypass this guard. It is off by default, the `http`/`https` protocol requirement still applies, and enabling it re-opens the SSRF surface the check protects against. Only turn it on for a trusted, isolated deployment.

## Troubleshooting

### Notifications show in Margaya but are not delivered

Check that:

- the global channel is configured and enabled
- a workspace rule exists for the notification workspace
- the workspace rule is active
- the channel is enabled on that workspace rule
- the project is included when using `Selected projects`

### Push notifications don't arrive

Check that:

- the card doesn't say push isn't set up on this server, which only your administrator can fix
- **On this device** is on, and notifications aren't blocked for this site in your browser
- on iPhone and iPad, you opened Margaya from your Home Screen
- **Notify me about** includes the event, and the task isn't muted
- if the workspace has a delivery rule, the rule is active, **Push notifications** is on and the project is included
- if your administrator replaced the server's push keys, you turned push on again
- if your account was signed out everywhere, or you reset your password, you turned push on again

### No notification sound plays

Browsers allow sound only after you've clicked or pressed a key on the page, so a tab you just opened stays silent until you interact with it. Check that:

- **Play sounds** is on, the kind of notification has a sound other than **None**, and the volume is above 0
- **Mute sounds on this device** is off, and you're outside your quiet hours
- the browser tab or site isn't muted, and the system volume is up
- **Notify me about** includes the event, and the task isn't muted

### Email is enabled but nothing is sent

Emails are batched, so the first one can take up to the batch window (5 minutes by default) to arrive.

Check that:

- SMTP is configured correctly
- the signed-in user has an email address
- the server can reach the SMTP provider

### ntfy, Gotify, or webhook save fails

Check that:

- the URL is valid
- the destination is reachable from the API server
- the hostname does not resolve to a blocked local or private address
