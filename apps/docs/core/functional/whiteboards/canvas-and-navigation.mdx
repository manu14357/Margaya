title: Move around the canvas
description: Pan and zoom a whiteboard, fit it to the screen, use the minimap, find text on the board, choose a background pattern and paper color, and snap elements to a grid or to each other
---

A board has no edges: it grows in every direction as you add to it. This page covers how to get around a big board, how to find things on it and how to set up its background.

## Pan

- Scroll with the mouse wheel or two fingers on a touchpad to move up, down, left and right. Hold `Shift` while you scroll to move sideways with a plain mouse wheel.
- Hold `Space` and drag, or pick **Hand (pan)** (`H`) and drag.
- On a touch screen, drag with one finger while **Hand (pan)** is active, or pinch and drag with two fingers.

## Zoom

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Zoom in or out | `Ctrl` `+` and `Ctrl` `-`, the **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** buttons in the bottom bar, `Ctrl` with the mouse wheel, or pinch on a touchpad or touch screen. The mouse and pinch zoom around the pointer. |
| Go back to 100% | Click the percentage in the bottom bar, or press `Ctrl` `0` (**Reset zoom**). |
| See the whole board | **Zoom to fit** (`Shift` `1`): the fit button in the bottom bar, or **Zoom to fit** in **More board options**. |
| Look closely at the selection | **Zoom to selection** (`Shift` `2`). |

Zoom goes from 10% to 3,000%. On macOS, use `⌘` instead of `Ctrl`.

## The minimap

**Show minimap** (`Alt` `M`, or the map button in the bottom bar) shows a small overview of the whole board in the bottom-right corner, with a frame around the part you are looking at. Click or drag in the minimap to move your view there. Your browser remembers whether the minimap is open.

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/minimap" alt="The Signup flow board with the minimap open in the bottom-right corner, showing the whole flowchart as gray blocks and a green frame around the visible part" caption="The minimap shows the whole board and where you are on it." />

The minimap isn't shown on phone-sized screens.

## Find on board

**Find on board** (`Ctrl` `F`) searches the text on the board you have open: text, sticky notes, shape and arrow labels, frame names and the keys and titles of task cards.

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/search" alt="The Find on board dialog with launch typed in the search field and two matches: Launch week and Launch review Thursday 10:00" caption="Find on board lists the elements whose text matches." />

1. Press `Ctrl` `F` (`⌘` `F` on macOS) while the board has focus.
2. Type a word. **Matches** lists up to 50 elements; "Nothing on this board matches." means there are none.
3. Click a match, or press `Enter` for the first one. The board scrolls to that element, zooms out if it doesn't fit, and selects it.

To find a board by its content from the list, use **Search boards** on the Whiteboards tab instead. See [Find a board](/core/functional/whiteboards/create-and-organize#find-a-board).

## Background

The **Background** button in the bottom bar sets the canvas behind everything on the board. The background is part of the board, so everyone on it sees the same one. On a phone it is in the **Style** popover when nothing is selected.

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/background-dots" alt="The Hero sketches board with a dotted background and the Background popover open, showing Pattern with Dots picked, the Spacing slider at Medium and the Paper color swatches" caption="A dotted background, with the Background popover open." />

- **Pattern**: **Plain** (the default), **Dots**, **Grid** or **Lines**.
- **Spacing**: **Small**, **Medium** or **Large**, for the dots, grid or lines. It has no effect on **Plain**.
- **Paper color**: **White**, **Cream**, **Stone**, **Mist** or **Mint**. In dark mode the board turns them into matching dark shades, so the board stays readable for everyone.

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/background-grid" alt="The Release order board on a mint grid background, with two task cards, a sticky note and the Background popover open with Grid and the Mint paper color picked" caption="A grid on mint paper." />

The pattern is only a backdrop: it doesn't make elements snap. **Export as PNG** includes the pattern; **Export as SVG** includes the paper color but not the pattern. Viewers and the public page see the background but can't change it.

## Snap to grid and to objects

- **Snap to objects** (`Alt` `S`) is on by default: as you move or resize elements, they snap to the edges and centers of nearby elements, and guide lines show what they line up with. Hold `Ctrl` while dragging to move freely for a moment. Your browser remembers the setting.
- **Snap to grid** (`Ctrl` `'`) makes elements snap to a grid of 20 units as you draw, move and resize them. On a plain board the grid shows while snapping is on, so you can see where things will land; on a board with a background pattern, the pattern stays as it is and the snapping works the same. It's a board setting, so it's on or off for everyone on the board.

Both are also in the right-click menu of the empty canvas.

## Full screen

**Full screen** in **More board options** shows the board without the rest of Margaya, using your whole screen. Choose **Exit full screen** or press `Esc` to go back.

## Present a board

**Present** in **More board options** walks through the board full screen, one frame at a time, without toolbars. Nothing can be edited while you present, but changes others make still show.

- The frames come in reading order: rows from top to bottom, each row from left to right. A board without frames is shown whole.
- Click anywhere to go to the next frame. `Shift`-click or right-click goes back.
- The controls at the bottom have **Previous frame**, **Next frame**, the position (such as `2 / 5`) and **Exit presentation**.

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/present" alt="The Beta release review board in present mode, filling the window with its What shipped frame: three sticky notes, Offline task lists, Push notification preferences and Faster board loading, above the line All three are in the beta build, and in the bottom-right corner the controls with Previous frame, 2 / 5, Next frame and Exit presentation" caption="Present mode shows one frame at a time; the controls say where you are." />

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `→`, `↓`, `Page Down`, `Space` or `Enter` | Next frame |
| `←`, `↑`, `Page Up` or `Backspace` | Previous frame |
| `Home` / `End` | First / last frame |
| `Esc` | Exit presentation |

If the browser doesn't allow full screen, the board still fills the window. Leaving full screen also ends the presentation.

Anyone who can open the board can present it. A link to the board with `?present=1` added opens it straight in present mode; exiting turns that page back into the normal board.

In the [desktop app](/core/functional/desktop-app), **Present in new window** opens the presentation in a window of its own, which you can move to a second screen while you keep the board open in the first.
