Boards live in a project's **Whiteboards** tab and in **All whiteboards** in the sidebar. This page covers everything you do with a board as a whole. For what goes on the canvas, see [Draw and write](/core/functional/whiteboards/draw-and-write).

## Create a board

- On a project's **Whiteboards** tab, choose **New whiteboard**. The board opens right away, named **Untitled board** until you give it a name.
- On **All whiteboards** in the sidebar, choose **New whiteboard** and pick the project under **Create in project**. Only projects with Whiteboards on are listed.
- In the command palette, choose **New whiteboard** while you are in the project. **Go to whiteboards** opens the project's tab.

Creating boards needs the **Create whiteboards** permission (members and up by default). A project holds up to 5,000 boards.

## Start from a template

Open the arrow next to **New whiteboard** (**More ways to create a whiteboard**) and pick a template under **Start from a template**. The board starts with the template's frames, notes and shapes and is named after the template. Everything on it can be changed or deleted afterwards.

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/template-menu" alt="The New whiteboard button with its menu open under Start from a template, listing Blank board, Brainstorm, Retrospective, Sprint planning, User story map, Roadmap, Flowchart and Kanban sketch, each with a one-line description" caption="Templates start a board with frames, notes and shapes already in place." />

| Template | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Blank board** | An empty canvas, the same as **New whiteboard**. |
| **Brainstorm** | A central "What are we solving?" ellipse, an **Ideas** heading and eight empty sticky notes in a ring around it. |
| **Retrospective** | A **Retrospective** title and three frames, **Went well**, **To improve** and **Actions**, each with empty sticky notes to fill in. |
| **Sprint planning** | A **Sprint goal** frame with a line of text to write the goal, and three frames below it: **Backlog**, **Committed** and **Risks**, each with sticky notes. |
| **User story map** | Four **Activity** notes across the top, a **Story** note under each, and dashed lines that separate **Release 1** and **Release 2**. |
| **Roadmap** | A **Roadmap** title, a long arrow for time, and **Now**, **Next** and **Later** frames side by side with sticky notes. |
| **Flowchart** | **Start**, a **Step**, a **Decision?** with **Yes** and **No** branches, another step, a document shape and **End**, all joined by connected arrows. |
| **Kanban sketch** | **To do**, **Doing** and **Done** frames to move sticky notes across. |

The template's labels are written in your language when you create the board.

## Rename a board and change its icon

- Click the name in the board's header and type a new one (up to 255 characters). Press `Enter` or click away to save, or `Esc` to cancel. You can also choose **Rename** in the board's menu.
- Click the icon next to the name, or choose **Change icon** in the board's menu, to open **Choose an icon**. Pick an emoji, use **Search emoji** to find one, or choose **Remove icon** to go back to the default.

Renaming and changing the icon need the **Edit whiteboards** permission, and the board must not be locked or archived.

## The board menu

Each card on the list has a **…** menu, and the board itself has the same actions in **More board options** (**…**) at the top right of the header. Depending on your permissions and the board's state, the menu holds:

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/card-menu" alt="The menu of the Launch planning card with Open, Open in new tab, Copy link, Rename, Change icon, Duplicate, Public link, Lock and Archive" caption="A board's menu on the list." />

- **Open** and **Open in new tab** (on the list only), and **Copy link** to copy the board's address.
- **Rename** and **Change icon**.
- **Duplicate**: a copy in the same project named "Title (copy)", with the board's current content. Duplicating needs permission to create whiteboards.
- **Publish to the web**, or **Public link** on a published board. See [Share and export](/core/functional/whiteboards/share-and-export).
- **Lock** or **Unlock**, **Archive** or **Restore from archive**, and **Move to trash** on archived boards.

## Lock, archive and delete

### Lock a board

**Lock** makes the board read-only for everyone, its creator included, so nobody moves something by accident. Use the lock button in the header or **Lock** in the board's menu. "Whiteboard locked" confirms it, the card gets a **Locked** badge, and the board shows the banner "Elena Marsh locked this board. Unlock it to make changes." **Unlock** in the banner, the header or the menu reverses it.

<Screenshot src="whiteboards/locked-banner" alt="The Sprint 14 retrospective board with a Locked badge next to its name and a banner saying that Elena Marsh locked this board, with an Unlock button" caption="A locked board is read-only until someone unlocks it." />

Locking a board is not the same as locking an element: to pin one element in place while the rest of the board stays editable, see [Lock elements](/core/functional/whiteboards/draw-and-write#lock-elements).

### Archive a board

**Archive** moves the board to the **Archived** tab. An archived board is read-only, with the banner "This board is archived and read-only.", and archiving revokes its public link. **Restore from archive** in its menu, or **Restore** in the banner, brings it back to **Boards**.

### Move a board to the trash

**Move to trash** is only offered on archived boards, so a board in use can't be deleted by mistake. After you confirm, "Moved to Recently deleted" appears and the board goes to the project's **Recently deleted** list with its content and images. Its public link is revoked.

To bring it back or delete it for good:

1. On the project's **Whiteboards** tab, open **Archived** and choose **Recently deleted**. You can also go to **Settings**, open **Projects**, select the project and open **Recently deleted**.
2. Under **Whiteboards**, find the board (**Search deleted whiteboards** matches its name).
3. Choose **Restore** to put it back on the **Archived** tab ("Whiteboard restored to Archived"), or **Delete permanently** in the row's **…** menu to remove the board, its content and its images for everyone. Permanent deletion can't be undone.

The automatic trash purge deletes boards that have been in the trash longer than the retention period (30 days by default). See [Trash and restore](/core/functional/trash-and-restore#restore-a-deleted-whiteboard).

### Who can lock, archive and delete

A board's creator can lock, archive, move to the trash, restore and permanently delete their own boards. People with **Manage all whiteboards** (admins and owners by default) can do it for any board.

## Find a board

On a project's tab and on **All whiteboards**:

- **Boards** lists the boards in use, and **Archived** lists the archived ones.
- **Search boards** matches board names and the text on boards: text, sticky notes, shape labels and frame names. **Escape** or **Clear search** clears it.
- The sort menu offers **Last edited** (the default), **Created** and **Name**, ascending or descending. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

**All whiteboards** also has a **Project** menu: pick a project to see only its boards, or turn off **Group by project** for one grid sorted across projects. The list refreshes every 30 seconds while it's open, and right away after your own changes.

To find something on a board you have open, use **Find on board**. See [Move around the canvas](/core/functional/whiteboards/canvas-and-navigation#find-on-board).
