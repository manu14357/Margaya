Boards work on any screen. On a small screen the editor rearranges itself so every control stays in reach, and on a touch screen your fingers and a stylus do what the mouse does.

## The phone layout

When the board is narrower than 640 pixels, as on a phone or a narrow window:

- The toolbar moves to the bottom, above the app's navigation, with **Select**, **Hand (pan)**, **Pen**, **Sticky note**, **Text**, the shape button and **Arrow**. The shape button opens the shape library directly.
- **More tools** (**…**) at the end of the toolbar holds the other tools: **Line**, **Task card**, **Image**, **Frame**, **Eraser**, **Laser pointer** and **Keep the tool active after drawing**.
- The zoom, undo and redo and **Keyboard shortcuts** buttons move to the top of the canvas, with a **Style** button (the palette) at the end.
- **Style** opens the Style panel in a popover. With nothing selected, it holds the board's **Background**.
- The list of people on the board, the minimap and its button are hidden.

## Touch and stylus

- Use one finger with the current tool: draw with **Pen**, drag shapes with **Select**, or pan with **Hand (pan)**.
- Pinch with two fingers to zoom, and drag with two fingers to move around. On a touch screen the zoom in and zoom out buttons are hidden at phone size, since pinching does the same.
- Press and hold an element, or empty canvas, to open the menu of actions, as a right-click does with a mouse.
- With a stylus, **Pen** strokes follow the pressure you apply. See [Pens](/core/functional/whiteboards/draw-and-write#pens).
- Buttons grow to a comfortable size for fingers on touch screens.

### Draw with a stylus

- **Palm rejection.** While the stylus touches the screen, and for half a second after it was last on or just above the screen, other touches are ignored, so the hand resting on the screen doesn't draw or move anything.
- **Draw with pen only.** On a touch screen, the **Pen style** row of the Style panel has a **Draw with pen only** switch. When it's on, fingers only pan and zoom, and only the stylus draws. The setting is kept on this device.
- **The eraser end.** Turn the stylus around, or hold its eraser button, to erase with the [Eraser](/core/functional/whiteboards/draw-and-write#eraser-and-laser-pointer) whatever tool is picked. Turn it back to carry on with the tool you had.

Tablets wider than 640 pixels get the full layout, with the toolbar at the top and the Style panel at the left.

## The desktop app

The [desktop app](/core/functional/desktop-app) shows your server's own web app, so whiteboards work there exactly as in the browser: live collaboration, task cards, exports and the public link. Keyboard shortcuts are the same as in the browser on that system. The desktop app adds:

- **Open in new window** in a board's menu on the list, in place of **Open in new tab**.
- **Present in new window** in **More board options**, which [presents the board](/core/functional/whiteboards/canvas-and-navigation#present-a-board) in a window of its own, for a second screen.
- Dropping files from your computer onto the board, as in the browser.

## On the public page

A [published board](/core/functional/whiteboards/share-and-export#publish-a-board-to-the-web) works on phones too: visitors can pan and pinch to zoom without signing in.
