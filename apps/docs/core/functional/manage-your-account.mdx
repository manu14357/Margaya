Your profile picture and your account itself are both managed from **Settings -> Account -> Profile**.

Your password, email verification and signed-in devices are under **Settings -> Account -> Security**; see [Account Security](/core/functional/account-security).

<Screenshot src="account/information" alt="The Profile page with the profile picture, full name and email, and the Danger zone with Delete account" caption="Settings > Account > Profile." />

## Change your profile picture

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Account -> Profile**.
3. Under **Profile picture**, click your picture and choose **Change avatar**.
4. Pick any image. It is cropped to a square and resized to 256x256 before it is uploaded.

Choose **Remove avatar** to go back to your initials. Pictures are stored in your Margaya database, so self-hosted instances need no object storage for this and a normal database backup includes them.

Changes to your name, picture, language and time zone show right away on your other tabs and devices, including the desktop app, and to everyone who shares a workspace with you, on tasks, comments and member lists. Nobody needs to reload.

## Delete your account

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Account -> Profile**.
3. Under **Danger zone**, select **Delete account**.
4. Type your email address to confirm, then select **Delete account**.

<Screenshot src="account/delete-account" alt="The Delete your account dialog asking you to type your email address to confirm" caption="Deleting your account asks you to type your email first." />

Deleting your account cannot be undone.

### What happens to your data

- Workspaces where you are the only member are deleted along with everything in them.
- In workspaces shared with other people, you are removed as a member. Tasks assigned to you stay and become unassigned, and time you logged stays on those tasks.
- Comments you wrote are removed.
- Your API keys and sessions are revoked.

### When deletion is refused

- **You are the only owner of a shared workspace.** Transfer ownership from **Settings -> Workspace -> General**, or delete the workspace first.
- **You signed in a while ago.** For security, sign out and sign back in, then try again.
