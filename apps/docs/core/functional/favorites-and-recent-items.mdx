---
title: Favorites and Recent Items
description: Pin the projects, cycles, modules, views and pages you use most for quick access from the sidebar
---

Favorites give you a personal shortcut list to the projects, cycles, modules, views and pages you come back to often. Margaya also keeps a lightweight record of what you open, in the background, with no action needed from you.

## Add a favorite

Click the star icon on a project, cycle, module, view or page (in its header, row, or menu) to add it to your favorites. The star fills in once it's added.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/favorite-star" alt="The Website relaunch project header with the pointer on the star button and its Add to favorites tooltip" caption="The star next to a project's name adds it to your favorites." />

## Use the Favorites list

Favorites appear in a **Favorites** section of the **Projects** side panel, in the order you set. Click one to open it directly. Drag a favorite up or down to reorder the list, or remove it from the star icon or its row menu.

<Screenshot src="getting-around/favorites-sidebar" alt="The Projects side panel with a Favorites section listing the Website relaunch project, the Sprint 14 cycle and a page" caption="Favorites sit above your projects in the side panel." />

## What can be favorited

Favorites cover projects, cycles, modules, [views](/core/functional/views) and [pages](/core/functional/pages). Individual tasks aren't favorited; use the sidebar's project and cycle/module shortcuts to get back to a task's context quickly instead.

A favorite is hidden automatically, without being removed, if its project is archived, the entity itself is archived, or the feature behind it (Cycles, Modules, Views or Pages) is turned off. It reappears as soon as you restore the entity or turn the feature back on. If the underlying entity is deleted, the favorite is removed for good.

## Favorites limit

Each person can have up to 100 favorites per workspace.

## Recent items

Every time you open a project, task, cycle, module, view or page, Margaya quietly records the visit (throttled to once a minute per item), so you can get back to it without doing anything. The **Recents** widget on your [Home page](/core/functional/home) lists them, and API clients can read them too.

## Permissions

Favorites and recent items are personal: no special permission is needed beyond your normal read access to the entity itself. If you lose access to something you favorited, it simply stops appearing.

## Tips

- Favorite the handful of projects, cycles, modules or pages you check daily rather than everything you've ever opened.
- Reorder favorites to put your most-used items at the top of the list.
- If a favorite disappears, check whether its project or the entity itself was archived before assuming it was deleted.
