---
title: Favorites and Recent Items
description: Pin the projects, cycles, and modules you use most for quick access from the sidebar
---

Favorites give you a personal shortcut list to the projects, cycles, and modules you come back to often. Margaya also keeps a lightweight record of what you open, in the background, with no action needed from you.

## Add a favorite

Click the star icon on a project, cycle, or module (in its header, row, or menu) to add it to your favorites. The star fills in once it's added.

## Use the Favorites list

Favorites appear in a **Favorites** section in the sidebar, in the order you set. Click one to open it directly. Drag a favorite up or down to reorder the list, or remove it from the star icon or its row menu.

## What can be favorited

Favorites cover planning entities: projects, cycles, modules and [views](/core/functional/views). Individual tasks aren't favorited; use the sidebar's project and cycle/module shortcuts to get back to a task's context quickly instead.

A favorite is hidden automatically, without being removed, if its project is archived, the entity itself is archived, or the feature behind it (Cycles, Modules or Views) is turned off. It reappears as soon as you restore the entity or turn the feature back on. If the underlying entity is deleted, the favorite is removed for good.

## Favorites limit

Each person can have up to 100 favorites per workspace.

## Recent items

Every time you open a project, task, cycle, or module, Margaya quietly records the visit (throttled to once a minute per item), so you can get back to it without doing anything. The **Recents** widget on your [Home page](/core/functional/home) lists them, and API clients can read them too.

## Permissions

Favorites and recent items are personal: no special permission is needed beyond your normal read access to the entity itself. If you lose access to something you favorited, it simply stops appearing.

## Tips

- Favorite the handful of projects, cycles, or modules you check daily rather than everything you've ever opened.
- Reorder favorites to put your most-used items at the top of the list.
- If a favorite disappears, check whether its project or the entity itself was archived before assuming it was deleted.
