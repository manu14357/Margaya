Margaya includes account and workspace preferences that improve clarity without adding noise.

## Open preferences

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Account -> Preferences**.

## Configure appearance

The **Appearance** section holds:

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark**, **System**, or the **High contrast light** and **High contrast dark** themes, which use stronger text, borders and focus rings. Margaya starts in the light theme until you pick another one.
- **Language** of the interface.
- **Time zone** (see [below](#set-your-time-zone)).
- **First day of week** for calendars and weekly ranges: Sunday, Monday or Saturday.
- **Default view** for tasks: **Board** or **List**.
- **Sidebar default**: keep the sidebar expanded on startup.
- **Customize sidebar** (see [below](#customize-the-sidebar)).

Your theme, week start, display toggles, calendar and timeline choices, task activity order, the pages formatting toolbar and your sidebar layout are saved to your account, so they follow you to every browser you sign in on. Each browser also keeps a copy, so the app opens with your choices before it reaches the server.

## Customize the sidebar

Open **Customize sidebar** from your account menu (your avatar), or from **Account -> Preferences**. In the dialog you can:

- reorder the main navigation (Home, Inbox, Your work, Projects, Pages and Analytics) and hide the ones you never use. Home is always shown, and **Settings** stays at the bottom;
- reorder or hide the sections of the **Projects** panel: task lists and views, favorites, and projects. Filtering the panel always searches your projects, even when you hid them.

<Screenshot src="account/customize-sidebar" alt="The Customize sidebar dialog listing the main navigation and the Projects panel sections, each with a visibility switch and move buttons" caption="Reorder or hide sidebar items; only you see the change." />

Drag a row by its handle, or use the up and down arrow buttons, which work with the keyboard. Changes apply right away and only you see them; they don't change the workspace's project order. **Reset to default** brings back the standard layout.

## Set your time zone

**Time zone** in **Account -> Preferences** is used for times the server writes for you: due-date reminders (which mention the due day) and email digests. Choose **Automatic (this device)** to save the time zone your device reports, or pick one from the searchable list. Until you choose, the server uses UTC. Task filters such as "due today" or "overdue", and analytics, count days in this time zone once you have chosen one (otherwise in your device's). Times on screen stay in this device's time zone.

## Configure task display options

Under **Display options**, toggle the fields you want visible in task views:

- **Task numbers**
- **Assignees**
- **Due dates**
- **Labels**
- **Priority** indicators
- **Estimates** on board cards

**Reset** next to the section title turns them all back to the defaults.

## Tune workspace and project settings

Also review:

- **Settings -> Workspace** for workspace-level details (see [Manage workspace labels](/core/functional/manage-workspace-labels)), including the workspace **Time zone** (see below)
- **Settings -> Projects -> General** for each project's **Project lead** and **Default assignee** (see below)
- **Settings -> Projects** for project general, visibility, integrations, and workflow (see [Outgoing webhooks](/core/integrations/outgoing-webhooks))

**Settings -> Workspace** lists **General**, **Members**, **Roles**, **Labels**, **Webhooks** and **Billing**, then a **Data** group (**Exports**, **Recently deleted**, **Audit log**) and an **AI** group (**AI settings**, **Agents**, **AI skills**, **AI memory**). You only see the pages your role and the server allow: for example **Webhooks** needs permission to manage webhooks, **Billing** appears only when billing is on, and the **AI** group only once AI is allowed on the instance. **Members** keeps its own address, so existing links and bookmarks still open it.

## Workspace time zone

Workspace admins set **Time zone** in **Settings -> Workspace -> General** (it needs the same permission as the other workspace settings). It defines "today" for everything the server works out by day:

- cycle status and day boundaries: a cycle ending on a day ends at midnight in this time zone
- project automations (auto-archive and auto-close)
- [repeating tasks](/core/functional/repeating-tasks) that are created on their date
- analytics, when a request does not name its own time zone

Pick a zone from the searchable list, or select **Use this device's time zone** under it. Nothing changes until you save. Dates without a time, such as cycle start and end dates, keep their stored values.

Existing workspaces start on **UTC**, which is exactly how Margaya behaved before time zones existed.

## Project lead and default assignee

In **Settings -> Projects -> General -> People**, anyone who can manage projects can set:

- **Project lead**: shown next to the project name in the project header.
- **Default assignee**: prefilled as the assignee when you create a task in the create dialog or the quick-add bar (remove it if the task is for someone else), and assigned to accepted intake requests, including public-board requests, that have no assignee.

Both must be workspace members. When someone leaves or is removed from the workspace, they stop being lead or default assignee of every project. Through the API or MCP, a task created without any assignee field gets the default assignee; sending an empty `assigneeIds` list creates it unassigned.

## Suggested baseline for most teams

- Default view: Board
- Show assignees, due dates, labels, and priority: On

Adjust once your team has 1-2 weeks of active usage.
