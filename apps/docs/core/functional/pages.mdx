---
title: Write Docs with Pages
description: Write specs, notes and docs next to your tasks, edit them together live, comment and watch for changes, and keep version history, backlinks and private drafts
---

Pages are project docs. Each project has its own page tree, so specs and notes live right next to the tasks they describe. Several people can edit the same page at once and see each other's cursors.

## Turn Pages on or off

Pages is a project feature and is **on by default**. To change it, open the project's feature settings and switch **Pages** on or off. See [Turn on project features](/core/functional/project-features-and-column-groups).

## The Pages tab

Open the project's **Pages** tab. The list has three tabs:

- **Public** - pages everyone in the project's workspace with page access can see.
- **Private** - your own private pages. Nobody else sees them.
- **Archived** - archived pages, ready to restore or delete. **Recently deleted** on this tab opens the pages you deleted, in the project's settings.

To the right of the tabs:

- The **search** icon opens a field that matches page titles. **Escape** clears the text; a second **Escape** closes the field.
- The sort menu offers **Last edited** (the default), **Created** and **Name**, ascending or descending. Your choice is remembered in your browser.
- **Filters** narrows the list by **Favorites**, **Created by** (the page owner) and **Created date** (**Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Older than 30 days**). The menu has its own search, and the button shows how many filters are in use.

Filters in use appear as chips under the tabs. Each chip has an **×** to remove that filter, and **Clear all** removes them all. When nothing matches, **Clear filters** resets the search and the filters.

Each row shows the page's icon, title and parent path, when it was edited (or archived), whether it is public or private, its owner, a star to add it to your favorites, and the page menu. A lock marks locked pages. On narrow screens the owner is hidden.

## Create a page

- Choose **New page** in the Pages tab. A page created from the **Private** tab is private; otherwise it is public.
- Choose the **+** in the page tree panel to add a page at the top level.
- Use **New sub-page** in a page's menu to create one under it.
- Use **Create page** in the command palette.

A new page is named **Untitled** and opens right away, so type a title and start writing. Long titles wrap onto more lines; **Enter** moves into the page body. The title can be up to 255 characters. Hover the area above the title and click **Add icon** to give the page an emoji or an icon. A project holds up to 5,000 pages.

## Page templates

Open the arrow next to **New page** and choose **New page from template**. Pick a starter (meeting notes, a spec or a retrospective) or one of your saved templates; the new page starts with its content, and you can change anything afterwards.

<Screenshot src="pages/template-picker" alt="The New page from template dialog with the Meeting notes, Spec and Retrospective starters and the saved template Migration notes for this project" caption="Start from a built-in starter or a saved template." />

To save your own, choose **Save as template** in a page's menu, name it, and choose where it is offered: **This project**, or **Every project** of the workspace. The template keeps the page's current content and icon; later edits to the page don't change it. Whoever saved a template, or anyone who can manage pages, can delete it from the template picker. A workspace holds up to 200 templates.

## Publish a page to the web

Pages can be shared with people outside the workspace through a read-only link. An admin first turns on **Public pages** in the workspace's general settings; it is off by default. Then anyone who can share projects can choose **Publish to web** in a page's menu and switch on **Public link**.

- The link has an unguessable token. Anyone who has it can read the page without signing in, and search engines are asked not to index it.
- The public page shows the title, icon and content without Margaya's menus. Mentions, task links, attached files and links to pages that are not published are replaced with neutral placeholders; a link to another published page leads to its public copy.
- Switching **Public link** off revokes the link at once. Publishing again creates a new link, so the old one keeps answering "not available".
- Making the page private, archiving it, or turning **Public pages** off for the workspace also revokes its link.
- Comments stay inside the workspace. A public page shows none of them, and visitors can't add any.

<Screenshot src="pages/publish-dialog" alt="The Publish to web dialog explaining that anyone with the link can read the page, with the Public link switch turned off" caption="Switch on Public link to share a read-only copy." />

## Public and private pages

New pages are public unless created on the **Private** tab. A **private** page is visible only to its owner. **Admins and owners do not see other people's private pages**, and anyone else who asks for one gets "Page not found".

The owner can switch a page between **Make private** and **Make public** from its menu. Only the owner can change who can see a page, and not while it is archived.

Private pages also affect nesting:

- Only your own pages can go under a private page.
- A private page can't hold sub-pages that belong to other people.
- To put a page under a private parent, make the parent public first, or move out the other people's pages.

A link to a private or deleted page shows as "Private or deleted page" for anyone who can't open it.

## Nest pages in the tree

The page tree panel shows the project's pages. Toggle it with the tree button in the header.

- Pages nest up to **5 levels** deep. A move that would push any sub-page past level 5 is refused.
- **Drag and drop** a page to reorder it or move it under another page. A page can't be dropped inside itself. Invalid drops are announced as "can't go there".
- Keyboard: pick up a page with Space, move it with the arrow keys, drop it with Space, or cancel with Escape.
- Expand and collapse a branch with its arrow. Archived pages are not shown in the tree.

Moving needs permission to manage the page, and the page must not be locked or archived.

## Write in the editor

Click into the page and write. The placeholder reads "Write something, or press / for commands", and an empty line you are on shows "Type '/' for commands".

The page header holds the page switcher (after the project name) and, on the right, who is here, the sync state, **Lock**, the favorite star, [**Watch page**](#watch-a-page), **Copy link** and the page menu.

**Formatting toolbar.** On wider screens an editable page has a toolbar above the text: a **Text style** menu (Text, Heading 1, Heading 2, Heading 3), then **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough** and **Inline code**, the three lists, **Quote**, **Code block** and **Callout**, and **Link**, **Table**, **Image** and **Divider**. On narrower screens the blocks and inserts move into **More formatting**. Hover a button to see its keyboard shortcut. Use the arrow keys to move between the toolbar's buttons. To hide the toolbar, turn off **Formatting toolbar** in the page menu; your choice is saved to your account. The toolbar is not shown on read-only, locked or archived pages.

<Screenshot src="pages/editor" alt="The Relaunch plan page with the formatting toolbar above it: the Text style menu, text marks, lists, quote, code block, callout, link, table, image and divider buttons, and the outline bars at the right edge" caption="The formatting toolbar sits above an editable page." />

**Slash menu.** Type `/` and filter by name:

| Group | Commands |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Text, Heading 1, Heading 2, Heading 3 |
| Lists | Bulleted list, Numbered list, To-do list |
| Insert | Quote, Callout, Code block, Divider, Table (3 by 3), File, Link to page |

**Formatting.** Select text to open the formatting bubble menu: a **Text** menu to turn the block into a heading or back into text, then link and the text marks. The shortcuts shown in the slash menu also work, for example Ctrl+Alt+1 for Heading 1.

**Code blocks.** Hover a code block to pick its language and copy it. A code block set to `mermaid` renders as a diagram.

**Embeds.** Paste a YouTube link and choose **Embed video** or **Keep as link**.

**Uploads.** Choose **File** in the slash menu to attach a file. Images appear inline and other files appear as attachment cards. Uploads use the same size limits as task attachments.

**Callouts.** Choose **Callout** in the slash menu to put the current block in a colored box. Click the callout's icon to switch its style: **Info**, **Warning**, **Success** or **Danger**. A callout holds paragraphs, lists and other blocks. In markdown a callout is a GitHub alert, so it reads the same on GitHub:

```markdown
> [!WARNING]
> Freeze starts on Friday.
```

`[!NOTE]` is Info, `[!WARNING]` is Warning, `[!TIP]` is Success and `[!CAUTION]` is Danger. `[!IMPORTANT]` reads as Info. A quote without an alert marker stays a plain quote. Callouts work in task descriptions and comments too.

**Images.** Click an image to select it. Drag the handles on its sides to resize it, or focus the right handle and use the arrow keys (Home and End jump to the smallest and full width). The toolbar above the image aligns it left, center or right, resets it to its original size, opens it full screen and downloads it. Double-click an image to open it full screen; in read-only views a single click does. The full-screen view has a **Download** button. Width is stored as a share of the text column, so an image keeps its proportions on any screen and in print. An image with a width or alignment is written to markdown as an `<img>` tag (`width="50%"`, `data-align="center"`); other images stay `![alt](url)`.

**Move blocks.** Hover a block, or put the cursor in it, and a handle appears to its left. Drag the handle to move the block. Click it for **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. From the keyboard, **Alt+Shift+Up** and **Alt+Shift+Down** move the current block and **Alt+Shift+M** opens its menu. The handle acts on whole top-level blocks: a list or table moves as one. The handle is hidden on read-only pages and on small touch screens.

**Tables.** Put the cursor in a table to show its handles: a grip beside each row and above each column, and a **+** on the lines between them. Drag a grip to move its row or column, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Click a **+** to insert a row or column there. The table menu also has **Move row up**, **Move row down**, **Move column left**, **Move column right**, **Header row** and **Full width**, which lets a table use the page's whole width. Tables with merged cells can't be reordered.

**Page links.** Choose **Link to page** and pick a page. If the linked page is later deleted or made private, the link shows "Private or deleted page" to people who can't open it.

**@mentions.** Type `@` and choose a member. Mentioning someone who wasn't mentioned before sends them a notification naming the page ("mentioned you in Release plan"), if they can see the page. You are never notified for your own mentions, and duplicating a page doesn't notify again.

**Full width** in the page menu widens the text column for everyone who opens the page.

## Backlinks

The **Info** panel lists **Linked from**: the pages that link to this page. It shows "No pages link here yet" when there are none.

Pages that mention or link a task are tracked too. When the project has Pages turned on, the task detail (both the side sheet and the full task page) shows a **Mentioned in pages** section listing those pages, each linking to the page. It only lists pages you can open, leaves out archived pages, and stays hidden when no page mentions the task. The same list is available from the Pages API.

## Live editing and presence

When several people have a page open, edits merge as everyone types and each person's cursor and name show in their own color. The header shows avatars of who is on the page. Up to 100 connections can be open on one page at a time; beyond that you see "Too many people have this page open right now".

A badge in the header shows the sync state:

- **Saved**
- **Not saved yet** - "We'll keep retrying. Don't close this tab."
- **Offline** - "You can keep editing. Changes sync when the connection is back."
- **Can't connect**, with **Retry**

### Offline

If the connection drops, you can keep editing. Changes are kept in memory in the open tab and sync when the connection returns. If you try to close or reload the tab with unsynced changes, the browser warns you first. Closing the tab discards anything not yet synced.

If the page was locked or archived, or your permission was removed, while you were offline, your offline changes are rejected and a message says why.

### Stored copy

If live editing can't connect, Margaya shows the last saved version with the banner "Showing the last saved version. Live editing resumes when the connection is back." Use **Retry** to reconnect. In this state edits are saved through a plain save request instead, and if the page changed elsewhere you see "This page changed elsewhere, so your last edit wasn't saved" with the latest version.

A page has a size limit. If it grows too large, a banner asks you to copy your recent changes and **Reload**.

## Comment on a page

Open the side panel and select **Comments** to discuss a page without changing it.

<Screenshot src="pages/page-comments" alt="The Relaunch plan page with the side panel open on the Comments tab, showing three comments by Priya Raman, Sofia Duarte and Elena Marsh, and a box to add a comment" caption="Comments sit in the side panel, oldest first." />

- Type in **Add a comment…** and click **Comment**. You can format text and `@`mention people, as in task comments. Mentioning someone notifies them, if they can see the page.
- The **...** menu on a comment has **Edit** and **Delete**. Only its author can edit it. Its author or someone with **Manage all pages** can delete it, and that can't be undone. A changed comment shows **edited**.
- You can comment on a locked page. An archived page keeps its comments but takes no new ones, until you restore it.

Page comments are simpler than task comments:

- They're one flat list, oldest first. There are no replies or threads.
- They attach to the whole page, not to a passage of text.
- They have no reactions and can't hold uploaded files.
- They stay inside the workspace. [Public pages](#publish-a-page-to-the-web) never show them, and workspace webhooks don't send them.

## Watch a page

Watch a page to hear about it in your [Inbox](/core/functional/inbox). The bell in the page header switches between **Watch page** and **Stop watching**. Anyone who can see a page can watch it, viewers included.

You start watching a page automatically when you own it, comment on it or are mentioned in a comment. **Stop watching** works for owners too. Commenting or being mentioned in a comment later starts it again.

Watchers are notified about:

- **New comments.** You're told once, even if the comment also mentions you.
- **Content changes.** You get one notification per page until you read it, and at most one an hour, so typing doesn't flood your inbox. People who edited in that same save aren't told.

Your own changes never notify you, and neither does a page you can no longer see. These notifications reach your Inbox and [push notifications](/core/functional/account-notifications), but not email, ntfy, Gotify or custom webhooks. **Notify me about** applies to them: comments follow **Comments and mentions**, and content changes follow **Other task changes**.

## Lock a page

The owner or a page manager can **Lock** a page from the lock button in the header or from its menu. A locked page is read-only for everyone, the owner included, shows a **Locked** badge in the header and the banner "This page is locked. Unlock it to make changes." Click the badge to unlock; "Unlocked" shows for a moment.

A lock blocks:

- editing the content, title, icon and width
- restoring a version
- moving the page in the tree or to another project

Still available on a locked page: reading, commenting, copying the link, markdown copy and download, viewing version history, duplicating, archiving, changing public or private, and **Unlock**. The Info panel shows who locked it.

## Archive, restore and delete

1. **Archive** hides a page, and all of its sub-pages, from the tree and moves it to the **Archived** tab. An archived page is read-only.
2. **Restore** brings the page and its sub-pages back. If the page's parent is still archived, it returns at the top level.
3. **Delete** moves the page to **Recently deleted**, where it can be restored until it is deleted permanently. **A page must be archived before it can be deleted.** Its sub-pages move to the top level and stay archived.

Only the page owner or a page manager can archive or delete. See [Restore a deleted page](/core/functional/trash-and-restore#restore-a-deleted-page).

## Duplicate and move

- **Duplicate** copies one page, without its sub-pages or version history, as "Title (copy)". You own the copy, and it appears right after the original with the same visibility. Duplicating needs permission to create pages.
- **Move to project** moves the page and its sub-pages to another project. Only projects with Pages turned on are listed, and the target project must have room for them. The page can't be locked or archived.

## Version history

Open **Version history** from the page menu (it also shows when the page was last edited), or the **History** tab of the side panel. The versions are listed on a timeline, newest first, under **Current version**, each with its date and time and who edited it.

- Versions are saved as you edit. Edits by the same person within 10 minutes update the newest version, and a different person or a longer pause starts a new one. Margaya keeps the newest 20.
- Choose a version to preview it, view only. **Back to current** returns to the live page.
- **Restore** replaces the content with that version. The current content is saved to the history first, so you can undo the restore. Restoring needs edit access to an unlocked page.
- Other people editing see "{name} restored an earlier version".

## Outline and Info

The side panel has four tabs. Open it with the panel button at the right end of the formatting toolbar, or next to the project tabs when the toolbar is hidden. On screens 1024px and wider it slides in beside the page and the text re-centres; on smaller screens it opens as a sheet. **Close panel** sits at its top left.

- **Outline** - the page's headings. Add headings to fill it. The heading you are reading is highlighted; click a heading to jump to it.
- **Info** - words, characters, paragraphs and reading time, plus who created and last edited the page, who locked it, and **Linked from**.
- **Comments** - [comments on the page](#comment-on-a-page).
- **History** - version history.

<Screenshot src="pages/side-panel" alt="The Relaunch plan page with the side panel open on the Outline tab next to the Info, Comments and History tabs, listing the headings Goals, Launch checklist, Owners and Open questions" caption="The side panel's Outline tab lists the page's headings." />

While the panel is closed, a page with two or more headings shows short bars at the right edge of a wide page, one per heading. Hover or focus them to see the outline and jump to a heading, or click them to open the **Outline** tab.

## Export to markdown

From the page menu choose **Copy as markdown** or **Download as markdown**.

## Print or save as PDF

From the page menu choose **Print / Save as PDF**, then pick a printer or **Save as PDF** in your browser's print dialog. Only the page title and content print, in the light theme, without the app's panels, toolbars, comments or block handles. Callouts, code blocks and images are kept on one sheet where they fit, web links print with their address, and embedded videos print as their link. The browser's own print command (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P) on an open page gives the same result.

## Who can do what

| Action | Viewer | Member | Admin | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See Pages and public pages | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create, duplicate and edit pages | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Read comments and watch a page | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add comments, and edit or delete your own | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Delete other people's comments | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Lock, archive and move your own pages | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Lock, archive, move, delete and restore any page they can see | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Delete and restore your own archived pages | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See other people's private pages | No | No | No | No |

These come from the workspace **Pages** permissions: **View pages**, **Create pages**, **Edit pages** and **Manage all pages**. Custom roles can mix them. A viewer sees the banner "You can view this page but not edit it."
