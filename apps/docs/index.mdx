## Why Margaya

Margaya follows a simple idea: **all you need, nothing you don't**. Every feature exists because it solves a real problem, and planning features stay out of the way until you reach for them.

- Define the work, assign clear ownership and move forward without ceremony.
- Use labels for streams like `onboarding`, `backend` or `customer-feedback`, and combine them with priority and due dates.
- Run it on your own infrastructure with Docker Compose, or connect your tools through the API, webhooks and MCP.

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/manu14357/Margaya).
